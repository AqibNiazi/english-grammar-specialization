# Simple Past Tense

Course 3 of the **Learn English: Beginning Grammar** Specialization.

## Course Overview

This course focuses on:

- Simple Past Tense
- Regular and Irregular Verbs
- Past Time Expressions
- Affirmative, Negative, and Interrogative Sentences
- Simple Past Questions and Answers
- Grammar Accuracy and Sentence Construction

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this course, learners should be able to:

- Use the Simple Past Tense accurately to describe completed actions and events.
- Differentiate between regular and irregular verb forms.
- Form affirmative, negative, and interrogative sentences in the Simple Past Tense.
- Ask and answer questions about past events with confidence.
- Apply the Simple Past Tense effectively in everyday conversations, academic writing, and IELTS tasks.
- Improve grammar accuracy and sentence structure for clear written and spoken communication.
